About Me

I am a senior Electronics Engineering student at Chung Yuan Christian University, focused on analog IC design and sensor interface circuits. My research project examines the electromechanical stability of multilayer graphene sensors.

For this work, I contributed to graphene fabrication, independently designed the analog readout circuit from architecture through schematic, and built an ESP32-based vibration test platform. I am especially interested in turning small resistance changes into reliable, measurable electrical signals.
