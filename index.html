About Me

I am a senior Electronics Engineering student at Chung Yuan Christian University, with interests in analog IC design and sensor interface circuits. In my graphene sensor project, I participated in graphene fabrication, independently designed the complete analog readout circuit, and built an ESP32-based vibration test platform. Through this work, I have become particularly interested in how circuit design connects small resistance changes to electrical signals that can be measured and analyzed.
